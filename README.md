# Critical Appraisal of Scientific Papers

Material for participants of the one-day workshop. It contains only what you need on the day.

## Files

| File | What it is | When you use it |
|---|---|---|
| [`participant-pack.pdf`](participant-pack.pdf) | Roadmap of the day (page 1), all exercise sheets, the quiz, manuscript A, the 7-layer checklist, the toolkit and the review report template | All day |
| [`slides.pdf`](slides.pdf) | The slides of the day | All day |
| [`materials/pdfs/`](materials/pdfs/) | TTM2 protocol (2019), TTM2 statistical analysis plan (2020), COMPare (2019, background for slide 9) | Exercise 1 |
| [`llm-exercise/manuscript-A.md`](llm-exercise/manuscript-A.md) | Fictional manuscript A, as a text file to paste into an LLM | Exercise 5 |

The TTM2 and Gautret papers, and their annotated versions used in the debriefs, are handed out on paper in class.

## The day

Page 1 of the participant pack is the **roadmap**: every step in order, with its duration, its slides and the pack pages to use. The day has a pre-quiz, five numbered exercises and a post-quiz. On the slides, each exercise has an orange START NOW slide; it stops when the time is up, and the slide says what comes next.

## Before the session

- Bring a laptop with access to one LLM tool (for example ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Copilot).
- No preparation is needed.

## One rule about LLMs

Manuscript A is fictional and written for training, so you may paste it into an LLM. **Never paste a real manuscript you review for a journal**: it is confidential.

## Keep after the day

The 7-layer checklist, the toolkit and the review report template are at the end of the participant pack. To go further, read the reporting guideline for the designs you review most (CONSORT, SPIRIT, STROBE, STARD, TRIPOD+AI, PRISMA); links are in Layer 1 of the toolkit.
